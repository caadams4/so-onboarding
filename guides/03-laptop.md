# 3. Laptop

## What you receive

- Engineering and Data & Insight: Linux laptop, built by Corporate IT.
- Everyone else: managed desktop image.

Your laptop is enrolled and reports its compliance status to the out-of-hours
IT desk, so please do not disable disk encryption or the endpoint agent.
Both are checked.

## First boot

1. Sign in with your directory login.
2. Change the temporary password.
3. Let updates finish. This can take twenty minutes. Make tea.
4. Set up the authenticator app (see the accounts guide).

## By office

- **Bristol:** collect from Corporate IT, third floor.
- **Rotterdam:** laptops are handed over by the on-site engineer on day one.
  The out-of-hours desk covers Rotterdam since April.
- **Toronto:** collect from reception; the Toronto office baseline is applied
  remotely, so give it an hour after first sign-in.

## Do and don't

- Do lock the screen when you walk away. Win+L or Super+L.
- Do keep the laptop with you or in the office lockers. Never leave it in a
  car.
- Don't install a second VPN client. If you think you need one, ask first.
- Don't store client data on the laptop. Use the engagement share.

## Lost or stolen

Tell Corporate IT immediately, any hour, through the out-of-hours desk
number on the back of your badge. Then raise a `CIT` ticket. Nobody will be
cross; the faster we know, the less there is to worry about.
