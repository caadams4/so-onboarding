# 1. Accounts

You get your accounts on day one, in this order. Corporate IT creates the
first two; the rest you request yourself once you can sign in.

| Order | Account | Who creates it | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Directory login (SSO) | Corporate IT | Username is `firstname.lastname`. Temporary password comes by text; change it at first sign-in. |
| 2 | Mailbox | Corporate IT | Address is `firstname.lastname@strikeonecs.io`. |
| 3 | Team chat | Self-service | Sign in with SSO. Join `#welcome`, your office channel and your pod channel. |
| 4 | Issue tracker (`jira.corp.strikeonecs.io`) | Self-service | SSO. Project keys are `PLAT`, `DEL`, `MKT`, `PPL`, `CIT`. You will mostly live in `DEL`. |
| 5 | Pathnote (`wiki.corp.strikeonecs.io`) | Self-service | SSO. Read-only until your pod lead adds you to a space. |
| 6 | Strikeboard (`board.corp.strikeonecs.io`) | Pod lead | Needed for engagement work. |
| 7 | Ledgerline (`time.corp.strikeonecs.io`) | Self-service | Timesheets. See the tools guide for the weekly cutoff. |
| 8 | Git (`git.corp.strikeonecs.io`) | Platform Engineering | Only if your role needs it. Ask in the ticket, see below. |

## Multi-factor sign-in

Set up the authenticator app before you leave the building on day one.
If you lose the device, Corporate IT can reset it, but only in person or on
a video call with your line manager present.

## Asking for access

Raise a `CIT` ticket for anything in the table marked "Corporate IT" and a
`PLAT` ticket for git or VPN problems. Put your office and pod in the
description; it saves a round trip.

If you have not received the text with your temporary password by 10:00 on
day one, tell your buddy. Mobile numbers on file are sometimes out of date.

Never share a password in the team chat, even with IT. IT will never ask.
