# 5. First week, day by day

Offices run slightly different welcome sessions, but the shape is the same.

| Day | Morning | Afternoon |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Welcome, badges, laptop handover | Accounts and sign-in clinic |
| Tue | Tools walk-through (`s1`, tracker, Pathnote) | VPN clinic with Platform |
| Wed | Meet your pod | Engagement brief, first timesheet |
| Thu | Shadow stand-up | Buddy session |
| Fri | Self-study, glossary | Feedback chat |
