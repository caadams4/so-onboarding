# Buddy checklist

For the colleague looking after a new starter. Roughly 30 minutes a day in
week one.

- [ ] Meet them at reception or on the first call
- [ ] Walk through the accounts guide; check they can sign in everywhere
- [ ] Sit with them while they create a VPN profile
- [ ] Introduce them to the pod channel
- [ ] Take them to lunch (or coffee, if remote)
- [ ] Friday: ask what was confusing, and file a ticket or PR for it

If something is blocking them for more than an hour, raise a `PLAT` or `CIT`
ticket rather than sitting on it.
