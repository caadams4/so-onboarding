# Glossary

## Pods

Delivery works in pods of four to eight. Each has a bird-of-prey code name.

| Pod | Notes |
|---|---|
| Merlin | Logistics engagements |
| Hobby | Cloud migration |
| Kite | Rotterdam-based, healthcare administration and quarterly client reviews |

Ask your pod lead for the current list; pods are formed and retired every
few months.

## Tools

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Strikeboard | Engagement tracker |
| Ledgerline | Timesheets and billing export |
| Pathnote | Internal wiki |
| Gaugeworks | Metrics and alerting front end, served at ops-monitor |
| `s1` | Platform Engineering's CLI for deploys, VPN profiles and logs |
| Strikeboard sprint | The sprint record in Strikeboard, not the calendar sprint |
| Tallyhook | Webhook relay that posts deploy and alert events to the team chat |

## Words you will hear

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sprint | Fixed-scope engagement of two to six weeks |
| Day rate | What we bill per consultant day |
| Bench | Not currently on an engagement |
| Stand-up | Pod's daily ten minutes. Not optional, but short. |
| CS | Consulting Services, as in `strikeonecs.io` |
